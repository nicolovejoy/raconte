What stays true in both options

  • The bar is one fixed height in every phase, so nothing moves when recording starts — that is the #53 fix and it is already pinned by tests that measure real frames.
  • Marks and Done are reserved, not inserted. In A they occupy the same row; in B, Done sits in the timer row and the marks flank the button. Either way the bar cannot change size.
  • Two voices is now visible without scrolling. Recent fits in B, and in A it is one short scroll away while idle.

Worth knowing before you pick

B is tighter but changes muscle memory. Putting the voice switch and ¶ beside the record button is the most thumb-reachable arrangement — it also means the two controls you tap mid-reading sit either side of the one control you must never hit by accident. A keeps them on their own row, further from the Stop button.

I lean A for that reason: 23% is comfortably inside your ceiling, and the Stop button keeps a clear exclusion zone. B is the answer if screen space matters more than that separation.
