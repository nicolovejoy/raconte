What this variant claims

  • Journal focus is app state, not screen state. The focus bar is one component, pixel-identical on Capture and Library — issue #18's designed switcher, built once.
  • Capture stays the landing screen. Open the app, you're one tap from recording into the journal you were last in — matches the paper-reading sitting.
  • Move-to-journal moves your focus. From entry detail, filing to Field Notes lands you in Field Notes' list with the entry highlighted (jump pill 5).
  • Recents = last three captures, compact receipts (one-line serif snippet), regardless of focus — a “did it land?” glance, not a browse surface. Browsing is the library's job.

Marker reachability

  • While recording, a fixed thumb bar replaces the scroll layout: voice switch · stop · ¶, all ≥76pt, glued to the bottom edge. Nothing to scroll, ever.
  • Voice button shows the active voice (opens in BN); tap = switch. Hidden when the journal's two-voices toggle is off (try Field Notes).
  • ¶ is always present while recording, single- or two-voice.

Honest to the real machine

  • “⚡ simulate interruption”: markers stay visible but disabled, stop becomes Resume — mirrors MarkerControlsModel.
  • Two-voices toggle locks once recording (pre-record only in this build).
  • Backdate carry-over is per journal: switch journals in the switcher and watch the pre-fill change.
