# Survey: voice-journal and therapy-adjacent apps (checked 2026-09-08)

Evidence base for `onepager.pdf` and `matrix.md`. Twelve apps in four categories, plus
Raconte's own column verified against this repo.

## How this was checked, and one important limit

All checks were made on 2026-09-08 from a Claude Code cloud session. **The session's
egress proxy blocked every vendor domain** (dayoneapp.com, journey.cloud, voicenotes.com,
otter.ai, reflectlyapp.com, rosebud.app, daylio.net, simplepractice.com, therapynotes.com,
quenza.com, apple.com/support.apple.com, apps.apple.com, play.google.com, and the Wayback
Machine). Every fact below therefore comes from **search-engine snippets of the cited
vendor page**, not from a direct read of the page. Quotes are as the snippet rendered
them; they are short, and each should be spot-checked against the live URL before the
one-pager is handed to anyone. Where a snippet did not carry a claim, the cell says
"not stated". Nothing is inferred.

Prices are US, as indexed on the check date, and the two marked "verify" came from
third-party pricing summaries because the vendor pricing page itself was unreachable.

## Category 1: general voice-journal and transcription apps

### 1. Day One (Automattic)

- **Platforms:** iOS, macOS, Android, Windows, web app. (source: https://dayoneapp.com/guides/day-one-on-the-web/web-app-faq/ and https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Day_One_(app), checked 2026-09-08)
- **Recording in-app:** Yes. Two iOS modes: "a standard recording (up to 3 hours, no transcription) and a transcription recording (up to 10 minutes, automatically transcribed to text)." Recording requires a Silver or Gold subscription; playback of existing audio works on any tier. (source: https://dayoneapp.com/guides/tips-and-tutorials/audio-recording/ and https://dayoneapp.com/guides/premium-subscription/day-one-premium-faq/, checked 2026-09-08)
- **Transcription:** Apple speech API. "On devices running iOS 26 with Apple Intelligence, transcription runs entirely on-device. On older OS versions or devices without Apple Intelligence, it is processed on Apple's servers." "an internet connection is required for transcription." Enable via Settings > Advanced > Transcribe to Text; iOS only; imported audio cannot be transcribed; not available on Apple Watch. (source: https://dayoneapp.com/guides/tips-and-tutorials/audio-recording/, checked 2026-09-08)
- **Audio kept after transcription:** Yes — audio is an entry attachment; transcript is added as text; recordings "stay playable on any tier." (source: https://dayoneapp.com/guides/premium-subscription/day-one-premium-faq/, checked 2026-09-08)
- **Where data is stored:** Device (usable without signing in, entries "stay local to that device") and, with an account, Day One Sync (Automattic servers). Not the user's own iCloud. (source: https://dayoneapp.com/guides/settings/account-management/ and https://dayoneapp.com/guides/premium-subscription/day-one-premium-faq/, checked 2026-09-08)
- **Encryption claims:** Verbatim: "End-to-end encryption uses military-grade technology (AES-GCM-256) to encrypt journal entries before they are sent to servers." "End-to-end encryption in Day One includes all media files added to entries." Default-on for new journals since v4.2 (Sept 2019). (source: https://dayoneapp.com/guides/day-one-sync/end-to-end-encryption-faq/ and https://dayoneapp.com/features/end-to-end-encryption/, checked 2026-09-08)
- **Account required:** No for local use; yes for sync, Shared Journals, book printing. (source: https://dayoneapp.com/guides/settings/account-management/, checked 2026-09-08)
- **Who else can read it:** Verbatim (privacy policy): "Day One offers End to End Encryption on journals, and if that option is enabled, it is impossible for our employees to access your journal data." Verbatim (E2E FAQ): "Day One never has access to your encryption key, which means your journal data cannot be read by Day One servers or by Day One employees." AI training, verbatim: "your content is processed only at that moment and is never used to train AI models"; "your entry content is temporarily decrypted to generate prompts and is never stored or used to train AI models." AI features are opt-in (Gold tier). (source: https://dayoneapp.com/privacy-policy/ ; https://dayoneapp.com/guides/day-one-sync/end-to-end-encryption-faq/ ; https://dayoneapp.com/guides/ai-features/ai-features/ ; https://dayoneapp.com/guides/labs/go-deeper/, checked 2026-09-08)
- **Therapist sharing model:** Shared portal — Shared Journals. Vendor copy names the use case: "a unique way for individuals working with a therapist, coach, or mentor." Caveat, verbatim: "members of a Shared Journal will be able to view, save, and even export all entries in a Shared Journal." Plus per-entry export/share. (source: https://dayoneapp.com/blog/introducing-shared-journals/ and https://dayoneapp.com/shared-journals/, checked 2026-09-08)
- **Export format:** JSON, PDF, Plain Text, CSV. JSON ZIP "can optionally include media, which will be organized into photos, videos, audios, and pdfs folders." Audio comes out. (source: https://dayoneapp.com/guides/tips-and-tutorials/exporting-entries/, checked 2026-09-08)
- **Price:** Basic free; Silver $49.99/yr; Gold $74.99/yr (Gold adds the AI features). Premium renamed Silver March 2026. (source: https://dayoneapp.com/guides/premium-subscription/day-one-premium-faq/ and https://9to5mac.com/2026/04/08/day-one-journaling-app-introduces-gold-plan-with-ai-summaries-and-daily-chat/, checked 2026-09-08)
- **HIPAA / BAA:** not stated. A site-scoped search for "HIPAA" on dayoneapp.com returned nothing. (source: https://dayoneapp.com/privacy-policy/ and https://dayoneapp.com/privacy-faqs/, checked 2026-09-08)

### 2. Journey (journey.cloud, Two App Studio)

- **Platforms:** Android, iOS, macOS, Windows, Web, Chrome OS. (source: https://journey.cloud/downloads, checked 2026-09-08)
- **Recording in-app:** Yes. Verbatim: "Record audio up to 10 minutes using the device's microphone. Audio recording is a paid feature." (source: https://help.journey.cloud/en/article/record-audio-via-microphone-9o05o4/, checked 2026-09-08)
- **Transcription:** None of recorded audio. Separate dictation only, via the OS keyboard's "Voice input button (microphone icon)" or desktop "Edit > Start Dictation" — i.e., the operating system's speech engine, producing text with no audio attached. Engine not stated by Journey. (source: https://help.journey.cloud/en/article/speech-to-text-p1dacg/, checked 2026-09-08)
- **Audio kept after transcription:** Not applicable — audio notes are stored as attachments and are not transcribed; dictation produces no audio. (source: same two help articles, checked 2026-09-08)
- **Where data is stored:** User's choice: the user's own Google Drive ("Users' data remains solely on their own Google Drive with no access granted to backend servers"), Journey Cloud Sync (vendor servers), or self-hosted Journey Cloud Sync. Hosting provider for Journey Cloud Sync: not stated. (source: https://help.journey.cloud/en/article/how-does-the-sync-work-1gaiaji/ and https://blog.journey.cloud/introducing-self-hosted-journey-cloud-sync-manage-your-own-journal-data/, checked 2026-09-08)
- **Encryption claims:** Verbatim: "Journey encrypts your journal entries by using RSA and AES (Advanced Encryption Standard) encryption." "Your data is encrypted during transmission using HTTPS and in Google Drive storage at rest." E2E is optional on Journey Cloud Sync: "your journal entries will be encrypted in the device before it is uploaded to Journey's servers." Caveats, verbatim: "Some meta data is excluded from encryption ... such as the display name of the drive, and the date and time of the journal entries." "you will not be able to use the Search function on Journey if you choose to turn on end-to-end encryption." (source: https://help.journey.cloud/en/article/how-does-encryption-work-in-journey-15prpqn/ and https://medium.com/@journeyapp/journey-cloud-syncs-end-to-end-encryption-what-is-it-how-does-it-work-e5f90c8c18e, checked 2026-09-08)
- **Account required:** Google account for Drive sync or Journey account for Cloud Sync; offline/no-account use: not stated. (source: https://help.journey.cloud/en/article/how-does-the-sync-work-1gaiaji/, checked 2026-09-08)
- **Who else can read it:** Verbatim (policy): "journal entries that use end-to-end encryption cannot be accessed by anyone, including Journey employees, other than the logged in author of the entry." Without E2E, staff access: not stated. "Journey does not sell user's journal data." AI (Odyssey AI, GPT-based), verbatim: "by default, users are opted out of Journey Odyssey AI"; "Journey does not employ users' personal data for training language model"; queries are "redacted" and "deleted after generating responses." Odyssey AI cannot be used on E2E-encrypted journals. (source: https://journey.cloud/policy ; https://journey.cloud/gptPolicy ; https://help.journey.cloud/en/article/odyssey-ai-m15z7d/ ; https://forum.journey.cloud/d/268-i-cant-use-odyssey-ai-because-i-encrypted-my-journal, checked 2026-09-08)
- **Therapist sharing model:** None specific; export only (plus "Publish" features). (source: https://help.journey.cloud/en/category/import-export-and-publish-1rixq78/, checked 2026-09-08)
- **Export format:** ZIP (JSON per entry + media) via Settings > Data & Cloud Services > Export/Backup; web app: Backup > New Backup. A community parser of the ZIP handles text, dates, location and photos; whether audio attachments are included in the ZIP: not stated. (source: https://help.journey.cloud/en/article/archive-journal-entries-to-zip-format-v6dsvi/ and https://gist.github.com/mbafford/2c18f5c4d7b0dab673fddb1af2126680, checked 2026-09-08)
- **Price:** Membership US$6.99/mo or US$49.99/yr; $199 lifetime; one-platform Premium license $17.99 one-time. (source: https://journey.cloud/membership and https://help.journey.cloud/en/article/journeycloud-membership-k32g8e/, checked 2026-09-08)
- **HIPAA / BAA:** not stated. A search for "HIPAA" scoped to journey.cloud returned no Journey pages. (source: https://journey.cloud/policy, checked 2026-09-08)

### Cross-app notes (category 1) for the therapist comparison

- Only **Day One** claims end-to-end encryption of audio and keeps a transcript alongside the original recording, with on-device transcription on iOS 26 + Apple Intelligence (Apple servers otherwise). **Journey** offers optional E2E but does not transcribe recorded audio at all.
- Only **Otter** publishes a HIPAA/BAA claim, and only for Enterprise; it is also the only one of the five whose policy states it trains models on user audio by default.
- None of the five offers a purpose-built therapist portal; Day One's Shared Journals is the closest, and its own copy warns members can export everything.
- Every price and quote above should be re-read directly from the cited vendor URL before publication, because this session could not load any vendor page directly.

### Cross-cutting observations (categories 2 to 4) observations for the comparison

1. Only two products keep audio and transcript entirely on the device with no vendor account: Apple Journal (device + user's E2E iCloud) and Mentalium (device only). Daylio is device-only for text/photos and says so plainly, but is silent on where its new "AI transcription" runs.
2. None of the three therapist-portal products (SimplePractice, TherapyNotes, Quenza) lets a client record audio in-app; Quenza is the only one with a client-controlled free-form journal, and the only one where the client decides per-note what the therapist sees.
3. Rosebud is the only consumer app that names a HIPAA posture, and it says "HIPAA-aligned" with BAAs to its own vendors — it does not offer a BAA to a therapist, and journal text is sent (anonymised) to OpenAI, Anthropic and Groq.
4. TherapyNotes' privacy policy carries an internal "improve algorithms, artificial intelligence, machine learning" clause on User Data, kept in-house; SimplePractice retains de-identified Note Taker transcripts for prompt improvement and states it does not train a model on them. Neither applies to client-authored portal content specifically; check each vendor's client/patient privacy policy for that.
5. The one clause a therapist might have seen quoted against Rosebud (licensing anonymised datasets to "frontier AI laboratories") is from a different company's policy (lab.rosebud.ai) and should not be attributed to the journal app.
