What this variant claims

  • There is no journal focus. Capture is context-free; the library's chips are the only scope, and that's fine because they no longer compete with a second switcher upstream.
  • Filing is a moment, not a mode. The stop→file card carries journal and backdate together, pre-filled from the last filing — a sitting is record, stop, tap “File to 1987 Journal”, repeat.
  • Recents is an inbox. Its one job is filing state: green = filed, amber = Unfiled. “Later” is allowed and visible forever after.
  • Move-to-journal lands in the destination list (jump pill 5) — same fix as the other variants, expressed through library chips.

Marker reachability

  • Identical fixed thumb bar to A: voice · stop · ¶ glued to the bottom edge.

Costs this variant pays (honest)

  • Two voices can't be journal-derived — the journal isn't known at record time. It becomes a capture-level chip carried from the last capture, not per journal. A real divergence from the shipped per-journal carry-over.
  • Backdate moves to after the take. A kill mid-recording means an entry with no backdate yet (user-journeys §8's open question becomes the normal case — recovery lands it Unfiled+undated in the inbox, which at least makes it loud).
  • Interruption/resume unchanged: markers visible but disabled, stop becomes Resume.
