  • Global focus: the bar under the title is the one switcher, identical on Capture ("Recording into") and Library ("Browsing").
  • Switcher sheet carries C's designed rows (cover, name, range, count, two-voices / locked tags) and opens the current journal's Settings — where #35's Easy / Confirm / Locked control lives.
  • B's receipt after stop; move-to-journal lands in the destination list with the row marked (pill 6).

  • Interruption (⚡), two-voices lock, per-journal backdate carry — all as shipped.

This treatment

  • Warm chalk paper (#faf9f6), near-ink text, one sage accent used only for meaning: confirmation, selection, interactive text.
  • Structure from 1 px hairlines and whitespace — no fills, no shadows inside the screen, 2 px corners on everything including toggles (square knobs).
  • Uppercase micro-labels (9.5 px, wide tracking) do the section headers; body text stays mixed-case and quiet.
  • Record stays a red circle in a square button — the one round thing, on purpose.
